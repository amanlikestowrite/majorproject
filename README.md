# Nyaya Sahayak (न्याय सहायक)
### Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice, Government of India

Official AI-powered interactive virtual assistant for the Department of Justice, helping citizens navigate judicial services, court procedures, case status, legal aid, and official initiatives.

## Features
- **Conversational AI Assistant**: Instant guidance on court procedures, case status (CNR), eFiling, traffic challans, and legal aid.
- **Service Discovery**: Quick access to eCourts, Virtual Courts, NJDG, NALSA, and Tele-Law services.
- **Voice Input & Audio Read-Aloud**: Built-in speech recognition and speech synthesis.
- **Universal Accessibility**: Conforming with GIGW 3.0 and WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
- **Privacy First**: No sensitive case records or personal litigant details are stored.

## Technology Stack
- Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript (No external frameworks or libraries).
- Modular architecture with standalone design tokens and component styling.

## Getting Started
Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
